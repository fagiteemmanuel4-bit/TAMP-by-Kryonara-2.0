Frequently asked questions

Who is TAMP for?

Anyone who wants remote work and is starting with no skills.

Do I need experience?

No. Every course starts at beginner level.

How long does a course take?

Nine weeks: three weeks each for beginner, intermediate and advanced.

Why do you ask for my NIN?

To confirm you are a real person so your certificate means something to employers. We store only the last four digits.

Can I rush through the topics?

No. One topic opens each day, so you build a real habit.

Can I go back to a finished level?

No. After you submit a level assignment, that level is closed.

How do I pay?

Online, or with a TAMP key from an approved vendor.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, before the cohort starts, and in full if we cancel a cohort.

How does verification work?

Enter the certificate ID on the verify page.
